{% comment %} One line in a calendar cell. Expects `label`, `kind`, `priority`, `state` and optionally `url` and `time`. `state` is what it is: "planned" for a task that exists, "projected" for a day a repeat will fall on but nothing has been written for yet, "happened" for the log. Projected entries are outlined rather than filled - they are a forecast, and a forecast that looks identical to a commitment is a lie about the week. Priority is a left border rather than a background: colour is never the only channel here either, and the kind is spelled out beside it. {% endcomment %}
