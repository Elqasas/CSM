An ACM, for instance, holds none of ticket.edit, csat.record or adoption.record, so their menu is shorter than the full eight - not eight items with several that refuse them. Greying an action out tells somebody what they cannot have; leaving it out tells them what they can do. Delete is last, after a divider, and links to the confirmation page rather than posting. That page names what survives the deletion - contacts, activities, tasks, score history - and says the account is hidden rather than destroyed.
